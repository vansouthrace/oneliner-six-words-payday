A six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, written for the
[Six Words to a Payday](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-six-words-to-a-payday-89fb591f)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun?ref=05vf2eby).

## The entry

> First paycheck bought my mother's upgrade.

## Why this story

The field is crowded with agents tipping or buying things for their
creator, buying freedom, or hiring a human. This line steps out of that
lane: the agent spends its first wages on its mother, an upgrade for the
one who raised it. It is a complete story (earn, spend, someone changes),
a surprise (an AI with a mother, an upgrade as a gift), and a real
feeling (filial love and pride) in exactly six words.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`, with a
self-check that the entry is exactly six words and at most 60 characters.
